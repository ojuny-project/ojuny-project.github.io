# Third-party assets

## Base16 color schemes

The site vendors palette data from
[tinted-theming/schemes](https://github.com/tinted-theming/schemes), pinned at
commit `3ac91137e879502c442ab89ba0f1a98077d0da9d`.

The collection is licensed under the MIT license. The generated data lives in
`src/data/base16.json`. The `everforest-dark-hard` palette is the default and
is also used by the Ojuny desktop.

## Desktop upstream

Ojuny configures and packages two upstream desktop projects:

- [MangoWM](https://github.com/mangowm/mango), the Wayland compositor.
- [Dux Shell](https://github.com/duxlabs-dev/dux-shell), the desktop shell still
  called DMS in the Ojuny codebase.

Their code and licenses remain with their respective projects.

## Ojuny assets

The logo is adapted from the Calamares branding in
[ojuny-installer](https://github.com/ojuny-project/ojuny-installer). The wallpaper is
from [ojuny-template](https://github.com/ojuny-project/ojuny-template). Both
repositories are GPL-3.0, matching this site.
